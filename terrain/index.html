Vibrotactile Indoor Terrain Perception

Classifying floor surfaces from wheel-mounted IMU vibrations, then rendering them as Xbox controller haptics so a teleoperator can feel what the robot feels. Team project with Tamzid Hossain and Stanley Nketiah.

What I did

  • Mounted a VectorNav VN-100 IMU on the wheel shaft of a Mecabot Plus robot to sense floor vibrations.
  • Recorded 40 test runs on tile, hardwood, carpet and grass with ROS 2 on Linux, producing 3,474 labeled data windows.
  • Classified the four surfaces in Python with 87.3% test accuracy and gave each its own Xbox controller vibration in Unity.

Method

  • Custom ROS 2 Humble driver parsing VNYMR serial data at 40 Hz on Ubuntu 22.04, recorded with rosbag2.
  • One-second windows with 50% overlap: RMS, variance, peak-to-peak, skewness and kurtosis, plus four FFT band energies across 1–20 Hz.
  • Compared an RBF SVM, a 100-tree random forest and a raw-signal 1D-CNN across a four-run feature ablation.

Elbow Joint Actuator for a Surgical Assist Robot Arm

One complete bolt-in joint module for a lightweight arm that holds a surgical tool or endoscope camera next to a patient. It has to hold position when power is lost, know its exact angle at power-up with no homing move, and stay small enough to keep out of the surgeon's way. Medical arms like the KUKA LBR Med set the reference.

Continuous torque20 Nm
Peak torque50 Nm
Range of motion±170°
Max speed30 rpm
Position error≤ 0.1°
Power-off hold≥ 50 Nm
EnvelopeØ90 × 110 mm
Mass≤ 1.5 kg

Inside the joint

  • MotorKollmorgen TBM2G-06008 frameless, B winding: 0.45 Nm continuous, 1.22 Nm peak
  • GearHarmonic Drive CSF-17-100-2A strain-wave set, 100:1 with near-zero backlash
  • BrakeMiki Pulley BXR-LE spring-applied brake on the motor side, so it only needs to hold about 1 Nm
  • EncodersMotor-side magnetic encoder plus an RLS AksIM-2 absolute ring on the output
  • Controlmjbots moteus n1 at 48 V in dual-encoder mode, with gravity feedforward
  • HousingMachined 6061-T6 aluminum, with every custom part buildable on a mill or lathe

First estimates

  • ≈ 52 Nmoutput with motor torque capped at 0.75 Nm. The gear sets the rating.
  • ≈ 40 Hzfirst resonance, so 8–13 Hz position bandwidth is realistic
  • 0.084°gear twist at 20 Nm, which is why the output encoder is there
  • < 0.02°expected error with the loop closed on the AksIM-2
  • 27%of total inertia on the motor side (0.216 kg·m² reflected)
